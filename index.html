How to play

  1. Get rid of all 98 cards. They run from 2 to 99. Two piles climb from 1, two fall from 100.
  2. Play at least two cards a turn, then end your turn to draw back up to eight. Once the deck runs out, one card is enough.
  3. Climbing piles take a higher card. Falling piles take a lower one. Any gap is allowed, but every number you skip is gone for good.
  4. The backwards trick. A card exactly 10 away in the wrong direction is always allowed: 42 on a climbing 52, 67 on a falling 57. Use it to win back room. Each ten has its own colour, so look for the same last digit in the next colour along.
  5. Drag a card onto a pile, or tap the card and then the pile. The line at the top shows how much room is left between the piles.
